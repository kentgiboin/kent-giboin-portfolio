How I approach transformation

Start with the business problem. Design for adoption. Measure the result.

My experience has reinforced that a successful technology initiative is not just a build. It needs a clear business purpose, the right stakeholders, appropriate controls and a realistic route into day-to-day use.

Business-first, technology-enabled

I enjoy translating between business teams and technical specialists: clarifying what needs to change, shaping practical solutions and keeping delivery focused on outcomes.

My background spans procurement, commercial analytics, data products, automation and integrated business applications across retail and aerospace.

01

Understand the problem

Clarify the business need, user experience and the value the change should create.

02

Bring the right people together

Connect business owners, users, architects, developers and delivery partners around shared requirements.

03

Build with governance in mind

Consider data ownership, intended use, storage, risk, access and controls as part of delivery.

04

Embed and improve

Use training, feedback and iteration to help solutions become part of how people work.

05

Show the value

Connect delivery to measurable outcomes, productivity improvements and commercial opportunities.
