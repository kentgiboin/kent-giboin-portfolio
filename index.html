01 / Enterprise transformation

Contract digitisation & commercial insight

Rolls-Royce · Led business delivery of an integrated contract-digitisation solution, with rollout to a procurement buyer population of 70+ colleagues. A high-value pilot led to an agreed US$800k supplier rebate.

Power AppsDataverseAzure DatabricksPython processingPower BISAP
Explore the case study
The challenge

Contract pricing rules, quantity tiers and index-sharing mechanisms were embedded in documents rather than structured data. Buyers lacked a practical way to compare actual SAP orders and payments with the terms agreed in contracts.

My contribution
  • Personally analysed a pilot contract, reconstructed its pricing and index-sharing rules, and compared them with SAP purchasing and payments.
  • Identified discrepancies that supported a supplier challenge and an agreed US$800k rebate. The rebate was agreed; receipt or credit was not confirmed.
  • Became the business project lead, coordinating a multidisciplinary team across application development, data architecture, ETL, SAP, project management and procurement.
  • Co-designed the Power Apps user experience in Figma with the developer and personally built the Power BI reporting layer.
  • Drove governance engagement, user testing, feedback-led refinement and rollout.
Outcome

The pilot provided the business case for a repeatable contract-digitisation solution. The wider solution was opened to the full procurement buyer population of 70+ colleagues. Team-delivered components included Power Apps, Dataverse, Azure Databricks, inherited Python processing, Power BI and SAP data.

03 / Commercial optimisation

Customer messaging channel optimisation

Sainsbury’s · Reframed a data-cleansing request into a scenario-modelling and optimisation project across customer messaging channels.

Problem framingScenario modellingPython optimisationCommercial analysisStrategic delegation
Explore the case study
The challenge

A category team needed help with a large, difficult-to-interpret dataset. The underlying commercial question was how much messaging should remain on SMS, move to WhatsApp or other channels, or stop as redundant.

My contribution
  • Diagnosed the wider commercial need and reframed the assignment from data cleansing into channel optimisation.
  • Proposed Python-based scenario modelling, agreed the scope with the wider stakeholder lead and set direction and expectations for the analyst delivering the work.
  • Resolved business-logic and message-classification issues when delivery became stuck, while providing proportionate oversight rather than taking over engineering.
Delivery attribution & outcome

Amanda delivered the data cleansing, dashboard, Python model and scenario design. The project presentation reported £528k saved to date and more than £2m of total opportunity, with the largest channel shifts still on the roadmap. The £2m+ figure is opportunity, not fully delivered savings.

04 / Big-data analytics

Consumables spend optimisation

Sainsbury’s · Built an analytical approach to expose outlier consumption and purchasing opportunities across a large, fragmented spend dataset.

Snowflake625k+ transactionsPeer benchmarkingSpend analyticsCommercial insight
Explore the case study
The challenge

More than 625,000 transaction rows across approximately £40m of annual consumables spend were beyond practical Excel analysis for stakeholders.

My contribution
  • Created a Snowflake data store and led the analytical approach, cleaning and normalising the data.
  • Incorporated store square footage and trading intensity to create more meaningful peer groups.
  • Used high-throughput stores as benchmarks to identify outlier consumption and purchasing-rule opportunities.
Outcome

The analysis identified approximately £300k of immediate savings through purchasing-rule changes and a further £2.5m–£3m opportunity for investigation. The £300k is identified savings; implementation and realisation were not confirmed in the source material.
